## 🛡️ Who We Are

**MR CYBER PULSE** is a legal, ethical security research collective focused on **OSINT**, **IoT security**, **vulnerability research**, and **compliance** for the Indian ecosystem.

We are a team of **builders, researchers, and ethical hackers** — backed by a network of **50+ experts** across cybersecurity, psychology, law, and digital forensics.

**We don't chase certificates. We build things that work.**

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

- Build **affordable, India-first** security tools
- Conduct **responsible vulnerability research**
- Provide **free, confidential support** to victims of digital harassment
- Train the next generation of ethical security researchers
